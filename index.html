Biomarker Discovery Pipeline

Research prototype

Metabolomics with machine learning, feature stability and reproducibility checks.

+

A research project I began studying and developing independently. Using MTBLS242, I compare metabolites before and after surgery and prioritize candidates for further study by combining statistical, model and biological evidence.

Work completed so far

  • Built a data preparation and modeling pipeline with participant-level splits and all learned preprocessing fitted within training data.
  • Compared paired effects, Random Forest feature importance, averaged rankings and models using all features.
  • Evaluated ROC-AUC, accuracy and feature selection stability through repeated cross-validation.
  • Tested Tier v0.1 with SHAP and recorded the reasoning behind each metabolite ranking.
  • Checked reproducibility across Python and RStudio using the same data splits.

Our project, Team 7, was selected as one of the 12 finalist teams in the KVIS Young Innovator Contest 2026.

PythonR / RStudioRandom ForestSHAPCross-validation

Status: exploratory research prototype. Classifying pre- and post-surgery time points does not establish a biomarker for clinical use.

Longitudinal Metabolomics Analysis

Analysis completed

Tracked metabolite changes over 12 months using matched participant data.

+

Extended the MTBLS242 analysis to baseline and months 3, 6, 9 and 12. Used 463 samples after setting aside entries that did not meet the analysis criteria, with sensitivity analyses to check the results.

  • Compared within-participant differences using Wilcoxon signed-rank tests and BH correction for multiple testing.
  • Estimated confidence intervals with 5,000 paired bootstrap resamples.
  • Created time-course plots, heatmaps and summaries of individual change patterns.
  • Checked results using sign tests, the Pratt method and BY correction.

Prepared a report on October 4, 2026, covering both overall trends and differences between participants.

Feature Selection & Evidence Review

Experiments completed

Tested feature selection stability and the limitations of the Tier rules.

+

An extension of the biomarker pipeline: compared five related studies, tested 900 conditions on real data and used 100 independent simulated datasets to examine how rankings change with the data, ranking method and evidence acceptance rules.

  • Added ranking methods and evidence acceptance conditions, recording the reasoning for each metabolite.
  • Reviewed biological evidence, metabolite identity, sample context and questions about signal origin.
  • Evaluated the benefits and costs of setting aside metabolites when warning flags were correct or incorrect.

The experiments have not established that Tier outperforms simpler methods using the same evidence. Results and limitations are reported accordingly.
